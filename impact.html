Where we fell short

Food security at 12 months came in four points under target. Our evaluation points to pantry hours in two rural regions and a gap in benefits enrollment follow-up. In FY2026 we are adding eight Saturday sites and a dedicated enrollment navigator in each region.

What we stopped doing

We closed a financial literacy classroom series after two years of flat results. The $340,000 it consumed now funds one-on-one coaching and matched savings accounts, which moved the savings metric 22 points in its first year.

Stories

Three families, three years apart

Names are shared with permission. Photographs are representative.

Education & Youth

Andre, 18 — Atlanta

Andre changed schools four times in two years while his mother moved between relatives. Bright Path kept him at the same tutoring site through all four moves. He graduated in the top quarter of his class and started at Georgia State this fall on a $4,200 RobinHood scholarship.

Workforce

The Nguyen family — Phoenix

After a restaurant closure took both household incomes, Kim enrolled in Career Launch logistics training while her husband picked up night shifts. She now coordinates inbound freight for a regional distributor at $26.10 an hour. Household income is up 71% since intake.

Housing

Denise, 54 — Cleveland

A hospitalization put Denise $3,100 behind and an eviction on the docket. Our staff attorney negotiated a payment plan the same week, and Safety Net cleared the arrears. Three years on, she is in the same unit, with a matched savings account and no missed payments.

How we measure

Our evaluation method, in plain language

Every program collects intake data, exit data and two follow-ups. Where it is ethical and possible, new programs run against a comparison group before we scale them. Nothing gets published without an outside reviewer signing off.

  • Independent evaluator reviews all outcome data before publication
  • Follow-up at 12 and 24 months post-exit, with a target response rate of 70%
  • Randomized or quasi-experimental design for every new program line
  • Program data, methodology notes and response rates published in full
